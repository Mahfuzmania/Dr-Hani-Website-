# Image generation prompts

Built-in image generation. Three separate composition boards; original photographs supplied as references. No production image replacements.

## 01 Signature

Create a high-fidelity, exceptionally polished modern website UI composition board for the real portfolio of Dr Umma Hani. This is a client design-selection mockup, not a coded page, wireframe, mood board, generic doctor template, or print editorial. Landscape image, around 2400x1600. Present a dominant desktop homepage view across the left 74% and one matching mobile homepage view on the right 22%, separated by a small neutral gap. Flat straight-on screens without laptop/device perspective. Show the desktop floating header, complete hero, the second section 'Her journey', and a glimpse of a photographic service feature below. On mobile the authentic hero photograph must be visible prominently within the first screen.
Reference inputs: image 1 is the supplied existing seated hero photograph, image 2 is a supplied dialogue photograph, image 3 is her clinical portrait. Embed these photographs faithfully as compositing inputs: retain her identity, clothing, photographic environment and all participants. Do not create replacement faces, invented people or stock imagery. No names for the other photographed person. No inferred endorsement, organisation membership or new clinical claims.
Fixed identity: warm white #FCFBF8, substantial deep navy #0B2038, supporting text #435160, restrained gold #A68139. Natural rich photographic colours. Comfortable blank margins on left and right: centred desktop content approximately 1280px inside a 1440px canvas, generous but controlled gutters. Text never pressed against corners. Restrained rounded photographic frames and tactile, polished controls consistent with the premium floating header/footer, not many separate cards. Excellent contrast. No beige haze, washed-out panels, decorative blobs, repeated divider-line rows, chapter numbers, unnecessary eyebrow labels, fake stats, award badges, testimonials or medical logos.
Exact name: 'Dr Umma Hani'. Supporting role: 'Physician · Columnist · Community leadership'. Short intro: 'Medical service, published perspectives and a life in the community.' Hero actions: 'Read biography' and 'Explore her work'. Header navigation: About, Medical work, Writing, Media, Gallery, Contact. Use an elegant Cormorant Garamond-like serif only where specified for identity accents; use a strong, contemporary Instrument Sans-like sans serif for normal headings, controls and body. Name must remain readable and fit deliberately. Journey copy can say 'Medical education at TMSS Medical College. Hospital experience in Bogura and Dhaka.' Keep other text concise, legible and factual. Professional and warm, premium and contemporary, photography-led.
COMPOSITION 1 — SIGNATURE. Put label '01 / Signature' outside the screens at the top of this board. Warm, refined, modern portrait composition. Desktop: cream floating rounded navigation with fine gold details and a raised navy Contact button. In the hero, place a compact strong left identity block and a substantial framed seated portrait on the right. Name in a medium-weight elegant serif, around 72px, with deliberate two-line composition and strong navy colour, not oversized thin magazine type. The role and short intro underneath use clear sans serif; rounded navy primary action and understated matching secondary action. Hero portrait is large, fully recognisable, subtly rounded at approximately 24px with careful inset breathing room; use the supplied photo, not a cut-out or a pill/circle mask. A small tasteful photographic caption may sit beneath the portrait, no fake credential panel. The whole hero has dimensional polish without an enclosing card. Journey below switches composition: smaller clinical portrait on the left, bold sans serif heading on the right, concise biography and two clean milestones. A dark navy service/photo feature begins lower down. Matching mobile: floating cream header, concise name, portrait appears before long copy, comfortable 20px side margins, two clear actions. Signature should feel personable, warm and convincingly premium.
