21 October 2026. This pass produces three image-generated desktop/mobile concepts and a correction plan. The user selects a composition before broad website implementation. The original photographs will be used in implementation, not generated replacements.

## Fixed requirements

- Public name Dr Umma Hani; existing seated hero photograph; authentic original and newly supplied photographs.
- Cream, deep navy and restrained gold; retain Cormorant Garamond, Instrument Sans and Bengali-capable typography, using a more contemporary hierarchy.
- Premium floating header and premium footer; matching geometry and finish across body controls and photographic features.

Selected photo/background bands may be wider inside a safe outer frame. Text stays about 60–65 characters wide.

## Three compositions

### 1. Signature

A warm, refined portrait composition. A substantial framed portrait balances a concise identity block; the name retains the serif as a recognisable signature. Strong sans-serif headings and controls give the rest of the page a contemporary voice. Rounded photographic frames, careful inset spacing and restrained depth connect the hero to the floating navigation. The journey uses the clinical portrait in a contrasting open split composition.

### 2. Immersive Navy

A stronger navy introduction with the existing event portrait integrated into a large photographic stage. Readable typography sits on a solid navy portion; the subject stays unobscured. The body returns to warm white, with well-framed imagery and alternating compositions. It has the most contrast and the strongest first-screen photographic presence.

### 3. Portrait Studio

A centred portrait composition with a concise name, deliberate side margins and supporting information placed around the photograph. Strong sans-serif hierarchy, restrained serif accents and clean photographic framing produce a contemporary studio quality. Mobile prioritises name, photograph, short introduction and actions rather than reproducing desktop side rails.

The options differ in placement, hierarchy, photographic rhythm and use of navy—not just colour or border radius. All three respect the same identity and factual constraints. The images illustrate direction; they are not implemented interfaces or evidence of production readiness.

## Implementation after selection

1. **Lock a visual contract.** Record the selected composition's desktop/mobile hierarchy, side margins, typography roles, image frame/crop rules, controls, radii, depth and restrained motion. Name any specific borrowed detail from another option. Preserve content inventory and the previous release for rollback.
2. **Complete the homepage foundation.** Implement the selected floating header, hero, journey and medicine section as one coherent composition. Put photography into the mobile first impression. Use the existing hero and original asset files; avoid aggressive crops and never cut off a companion to force a frame.
3. **Complete the homepage story.** Follow with dialogues, community/public life, selected writing, interviews/recent activity and contact. Remove repeated chapter labels and duplicated explanations. Give each section a distinct composition. Keep concise factual captions; fuller descriptions remain in archives. Preserve access to all approved photographs and videos.
4. **Apply the system to supporting pages.** About gets a readable biography and complete timeline; Writing gets refined searchable publication rows; Media keeps reports separate from authored work; Gallery keeps filters and accessible full-image viewing; Updates/detail, Privacy and error pages inherit the same typography, margins and controls. Preserve existing URLs and anchors.
5. **Verify photograph context.** Maintain a per-image record of original file, placement, event/date, caption, names, role at the time and supporting URL. Names require evidence tied to the exact image or client confirmation. Event attendance alone cannot name a companion. Courtesy visits remain separate occasions unless evidence links them. Do not imply endorsement, partnership or membership from a posed photograph.
6. **Complete one consolidated acceptance pass.** Inspect desktop/mobile together at 360, 390, 430, 768, 1366 and 1920px. Check visual consistency, comfortable margins, crops, portrait visibility, Bengali text, metadata, form contrast, button wrapping, keyboard navigation, dialogs, search/filters, video playback, empty/error states and all preserved routes. Fix the findings together; perform one final confirmation pass.
7. **Technical and release verification.** Run frontend/backend type checks, lint, production builds and relevant CMS/content/contact integration checks. Confirm no internal notes or drafts in public HTML/API/sitemap. Verify actual review deployment headers, noindex/robots, production canonical URLs, image delivery and browser errors. Preserve the distinction between technical checks and the user's visual approval.
8. **Deliver the selected direction as one Vercel preview.** Include desktop/mobile screenshots, the preservation checklist and remaining operational dependencies. Collect design and content feedback together. Production deployment requires explicit approval of this concrete preview. Production CMS migration/import remains a separate backed-up operation and must not publish drafts automatically.
